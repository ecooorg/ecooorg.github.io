SYSTEMIC PROGRESS REGENERATION PROTOCOL (SPRP)

ZenodoTDCommons

1. Purpose and Scope

This protocol defines the mandatory rules for the creation, evaluation, acceptance, and long-term stewardship of engineering work by parties that voluntarily adopt it.

The protocol exists to counteract a fundamental process: the spontaneous decay of civilizationally critical engineering knowledge. Its objective is not the production of superior individual artifacts. Its objective is the sustained growth of the survivable mass of engineering knowledge — the quantity of knowledge that remains comprehensible, reproducible, and extensible after complete institutional rupture, generational turnover, and loss of external infrastructure.

The protocol claims no universal authority. It applies only to nodes and Inheritance Lines that have explicitly accepted its terms.

Detailed computational methods, parameter values, and procedural regulations are maintained in companion specifications. In the event of conflict, the normative requirements of this document prevail.

2. Foundational Premises

2.1 Law of Amnesic Drift

In the absence of specialized, continuously enforced inheritance mechanisms and a periodically verified minimal executable core, the net rate of accumulation of civilizationally critical engineering knowledge is negative on time scales of 20–40 years.

Theoretical basis:

Each generational or institutional transition introduces irreversible loss of tacit and contextual knowledge.

Local optimization for novelty and speed systematically favours Line Breaks over Line Continuity.

Without countervailing forced inheritance pressure, the entropy of the technical knowledge base increases faster than new structured knowledge is added.

Empirical indications (non-exhaustive):

Repeated full rewrites of working industrial control, power electronics, and tooling systems that result in higher long-term stewardship cost and lower transparency.

Progressive loss of ability to reproduce or modify critical hardware/software stacks once original teams disperse.

Documented growth of “dark knowledge” — functionally essential information that exists only in undocumented tribal memory.

Observed increase in time-to-recovery and decrease in functional completeness when organizations attempt to reconstruct systems from surviving artifacts alone after major staff or vendor discontinuity.

Within this protocol the Law of Amnesic Drift is treated as a working axiom. All normative mechanisms are derived as necessary countermeasures. Refinement of the Law is possible only through the governance process and must be supported by both formal argument and longitudinal data.

2.2 The fundamental unit of progress is not the project, the artifact, or the repository.

The fundamental unit is the Inheritance Line — an explicitly maintained, branchable, and measurable trajectory of constructive properties, interface contracts, physical and logical constraints, and verification procedures across time.

2.3 Progress is defined exclusively as the growth of survivable knowledge mass carried by living Inheritance Lines.

2.4 Coupled Inheritance Regime

A distinct operational regime exists in which the marginal cost of producing new high-Validity nodes decreases as the density, connectivity, and verification depth of the Inheritance Line network increase. This regime is called Coupled Inheritance.

Characteristic features:

New high-V contributions increasingly take the form of low-cost branches and recombinations rather than costly independent constructions.

The Adversarial Contour more frequently fails to find substantially superior alternatives because the existing line network is already near-local-optimum.

Average |V| of accepted contributions rises while the effort required to reach the acceptance threshold falls.

Survivable Knowledge Mass begins to exhibit superlinear growth relative to active contributor effort.

2.4.1 Observable Indicators and Entry Criteria

The network shall be considered to be approaching or entering the Coupled Inheritance regime when at least three of the following five indicators are sustained over a 24-month period:

Median effort (person-months) required to reach |V| ≥ 0.78 declines by ≥ 25 % compared with the first 24 months of Phase 0.

Fraction of accepted contributions that are classified as “branch/recombination” rather than “new line” exceeds 60 %.

Adversarial Contour success rate (finding superior continuations) falls below 12 %.

Average number of live upstream dependencies per new high-V node increases while average time-to-permeability decreases.

Measured growth rate of Survivable Knowledge Mass (via Tech-Stack recovery metrics and line-coverage metrics) exceeds linear projection based on active contributor count.

Crossing into this regime is a primary success criterion of the protocol. Failure to show movement toward these indicators within the first five years shall trigger mandatory review of the normative mechanisms.

2.5 Participants in Phase 0 are obligated to collect and publish longitudinal data that can confirm, refine, or challenge the Law of Amnesic Drift and the indicators of Coupled Inheritance.

3. Core Definitions

Inheritance Line**: A durable, versioned chain of technical decisions that preserves and accumulates constructive power. A line may branch; branches remain accountable to their parent lineage unless formally and justifiably forked.

Survivable Knowledge Mass**: The volume of engineering knowledge that can be recovered and put back into productive use by a small competent group relying primarily on local resources after total external collapse.

Line Break**: Any action that terminates or renders unrecoverable an existing Inheritance Line without creating a demonstrably superior continuing line.

Civilization Tech-Stack**: The minimal nuclear bundle of Inheritance Lines sufficient to restore critical life-support and industrial functions.

4. Normative Principles

4.1 Inheritance is the default mode of work.

A pure green-field action is permitted only when accompanied by an open, reproducible proof that no existing line can be extended to meet the requirement.

4.2 Every Line Break is taxed.

The tax consists of a mandatory, proportional contribution of effort and resources toward the strengthening, documentation, and opening of the nearest existing lines of comparable function. Uncompensated Line Breaks receive the lowest possible resource and reputation priority.

4.3 Interface stability and branchability of lines take precedence over local optimality of any single node.

4.4 Every line must remain permeable: a competent engineer equipped only with a basic toolset must be able to diagnose, reconstruct, and continue the line without access to the original authors or external networks.

4.5 Every line must retain functional autonomy and local reproducibility after complete loss of external connectivity and partial loss of documentation.

4.6 Informational density of all artifacts belonging to a line shall be maximized. Marketing, administrative, and rhetorical ballast is to be removed.

4.7 The Civilization Tech-Stack shall be maintained as a living, executable nuclear bundle of lines and shall be subjected to regular public recovery exercises.

5. Validity Vector for Inheritance Lines

Every proposed contribution is evaluated by the vector

V = [P₁, P₂, P₃, P₄, P₅], each component ∈ [0, 1].

P₁ — Line Continuity & Strengthening**

Degree to which the contribution extends, densifies, or legitimately branches existing high-value lines, together with the quality of any proof that a Line Break is unavoidable.

P₂ — Branchability**

Ability of the line to support clean, low-cost future branching and recombination.

P₃ — Permeability**

Completeness with which the line can be understood and continued using only basic tools and the artifacts themselves.

P₄ — Autonomy**

Measured ability of the line to preserve function and reproducibility under network isolation and partial documentation loss.

P₅ — Density**

Quantity of recoverable critical function per unit of artifact volume after ballast removal.

Measurement requirements

Each component shall be computed by at least two independent methods, one of which must be static artifact analysis and one of which must be behavioral recovery testing.

Divergence between methods above a defined tolerance blocks the contribution until resolved.

Acceptance threshold: magnitude |V| > 0.75 and no single component below 0.40.

Under isolation or emergency conditions the relative weight of P₄ becomes dominant.

As the network approaches the Coupled Inheritance regime, the Metrics Framework shall be reviewed for possible elevation of the acceptance threshold.

Exact computational definitions, weighting functions, and tolerance values are maintained in the separate Metrics Framework Specification. That specification must keep all metrics monotonic, resistant to gaming, and jointly verifiable by static and behavioral means. Changes to the Metrics Framework are subject to the governance rules of this protocol.

6. Adversarial Line Contour

Every non-trivial contribution is subjected to a mandatory adversarial process: an independent party or automated system attempts to satisfy the same need by extending existing high-value lines more densely and autonomously.

If the adversarial continuation achieves superior combined scores on P₂, P₄, and P₅ at equal or better P₁, the original contribution is penalized or returned for revision.

7. Civilization Tech-Stack and Recovery Exercises

The Tech-Stack is the minimal set of Inheritance Lines whose joint survivable knowledge mass is sufficient to restore energy, water, basic manufacturing, communications, and emergency medical capability.

Requirements

All constituent artifacts must support fully offline reconstruction.

Public recovery exercises are mandatory and periodic.

Each exercise is performed by a group of limited size operating under pre-declared resource constraints.

The primary published metric is time-to-function for a predefined set of critical capabilities, together with the percentage of functional completeness achieved.

Full results, including failures and discovered line gaps, shall be published without exception.

An unsuccessful exercise automatically triggers review and, if necessary, revision of the affected nuclear lines.

Recovery Exercise results constitute primary empirical input for evaluating both the Law of Amnesic Drift and progress toward Coupled Inheritance.

Detailed scenarios, scoring rules, and logistical procedures are maintained in the separate Recovery Exercise Regulation. The normative requirements above take precedence over any procedural detail.

Changes to the nuclear lines of the Tech-Stack require supermajority approval and subsequent successful recovery exercises.

8. Deployment Phases

Phase 0 (minimum 36 months)

Work is restricted to three domains only:

open power electronics,

modular autonomous energy systems,

basic means of production (machine tools, additive systems, industrial controllers).

Advancement beyond Phase 0 is permitted solely after sustained satisfaction of the Validity Vector and Adversarial Contour on real lines and after at least two successful public Tech-Stack recovery exercises.

9. Governance, Reputation, and Anti-Capture

Reputation is accrued only through verified contributions to living Inheritance Lines.

Reputation decays with sustained inactivity. Contributions that permanently strengthen nuclear lines of the Civilization Tech-Stack decay substantially more slowly than ordinary contributions.

Exact decay functions, coefficients, and methods for proving nuclear-line strengthening are defined in the separate Reputation Dynamics Specification.

Additional rules:

Any participant above a reputation threshold may impose a 14-day stay on a disputed contribution. Extension requires broad consensus.

Institutional data flooding is algorithmically damped.

Exit with one’s own data and reputation is always possible; technical lock-in is forbidden.

Regular red-team attempts to capture the metrics, the ontology of lines, or the Tech-Stack are mandatory; findings are public.

Closed proprietary development is not prohibited. The protocol is designed so that, over time, the total cost of ownership and existential risk of closed lines become uncompetitive relative to lines maintained under these rules.

10. Obligations of Participants

A participant shall:

Audit existing personal and accessible archives.

Remove all non-technical ballast.

Explicitly map Inheritance Lines and inter-line dependencies.

Compute a preliminary Validity Vector for proposed work according to the current Metrics Framework.

Record the result on offline media (Cognitive Passport).

Confine initial practical work to Phase 0 domains.

Contribute to the longitudinal data collection required by section 2.5.

Identity inside the protocol is determined solely by the verified topology and quality of one’s contributions to Inheritance Lines. Credentials, titles, age, and nationality are irrelevant.

11. Companion Specifications

The following living documents form an integral part of the operational system but are deliberately separated from this normative core:

Metrics Framework Specification

Reputation Dynamics Specification

Recovery Exercise Regulation

Longitudinal Observation Framework

These documents may evolve at a higher rate than the present protocol. Any change that would weaken the normative requirements stated herein is void.

12. Success Criteria (First Five Years)

At least 70 % of new nodes accepted in Phase 0 domains achieve |V| ≥ 0.78.

Measurable reduction in average long-term stewardship cost relative to comparable non-protocol green-field work.

At least four successful public Tech-Stack recovery exercises with documented time-to-function and completeness metrics.

Adversarial Contour produces demonstrably superior line continuations in at least 20 % of cases.

Clear evidence of movement toward the Coupled Inheritance regime according to the indicators defined in 2.4.1.

Longitudinal data sufficient to support, refine, or challenge the Law of Amnesic Drift have been published.

13. Closing Statement

Under the Law of Amnesic Drift, unconstrained technical activity produces net knowledge loss.

The only known counter-process is the deliberate cultivation of dense, permeable, autonomous Inheritance Lines.

When these lines reach sufficient density and connectivity, the system can cross into the Coupled Inheritance regime, in which the cost of further high-quality progress begins to fall.

Progress is the growth of survivable knowledge mass.

All other metrics are secondary.

Violation of this protocol is not externally punished. It merely excludes the violator from the resource, reputation, and priority flows of the network that has chosen to resist amnesic drift.

Appendix 1: Plain-Language Guide to the SPRP

Why this protocol exists

Modern engineering and scientific knowledge is surprisingly fragile. It disappears when teams change, key people leave, companies shut down, or tools and platforms become obsolete. Systems that worked well 15–25 years ago often cannot be reproduced or continued today without enormous effort. We repeatedly rewrite working solutions from scratch because the previous ones have turned into dark knowledge.

SPRP exists to counteract this decay. Its goal is not to produce more elegant individual projects. Its goal is to increase the amount of engineering knowledge that remains understandable, reproducible, and extensible after institutional rupture, generational turnover, and loss of external infrastructure.

Core idea

The basic unit of progress is not the project, the repository, or the artifact. The basic unit is the Inheritance Line — a durable, explicitly maintained chain of technical decisions, interface contracts, constraints, and verification methods that can be understood and continued even when the original authors and external infrastructure are gone.

Progress is defined as the growth of survivable knowledge mass carried by such living lines. Any action that terminates or renders an existing line unrecoverable without creating a demonstrably superior continuation is a Line Break. Line Breaks are costly and must be compensated.

Main rules

Inheritance is the default

You are required to make a serious attempt to extend and improve an existing line before starting from scratch. A clean-slate rewrite is allowed only when you can provide a clear, reproducible demonstration that no suitable existing line can be extended. Personal preference or taste is not sufficient justification.

Line Breaks require compensation

If you abandon a working line and start over, you must invest roughly equivalent effort into strengthening, clarifying, and opening the nearest existing lines of comparable function. Walking away after a clean break without compensation is treated as low-priority behaviour within the network that adopts this protocol.

Lines must stay understandable

A competent engineer using only a basic toolset must be able to diagnose, reconstruct, and continue the line without the original authors and without external networks.

Lines must remain functional in isolation

The line should preserve the ability to function and be reproduced after loss of external connectivity and partial loss of documentation.

Evaluation focuses on survivability, not local elegance

Contributions are assessed by a Validity Vector with five components:

Continuity & strengthening of existing lines

Branchability

Understandability (permeability)

Autonomy (isolation resistance)

Density of recoverable function

Early on, precise numerical scoring is less important than honestly applying these criteria. Direction matters more than perfect measurement. Formal measurement methods, including defined acceptance thresholds and independent verification techniques, will mature with experience and will be tightened as the network develops. Throughout, public recovery exercises remain the mandatory empirical check against self-deception, already from Phase 0.

Public recovery exercises are mandatory

Small groups must periodically attempt to restore critical functions under pre-declared resource limits using only what the lines actually contain. These exercises are the primary empirical check against self-deception. Results, including failures and gaps, are published. Successful recovery exercises are required before the protocol expands beyond its initial domains.

Phase 0 restriction

Work under the protocol is initially limited to three domains only:

Power electronics

Modular autonomous energy systems

Basic means of production (machine tools, additive systems, industrial controllers)

This restriction is deliberate. The community must first gain concrete experience and produce working examples before expanding the scope.

What a participant actually does

Audit existing personal and accessible archives

Remove non-technical fluff

Explicitly map the Inheritance Lines and dependencies involved

Assess the work against the five criteria above

Maintain a compact cognitive passport on offline-capable media

Stay within Phase 0 domains at the beginning

Contribute to the collective record of what works and what fails

Use of modern tools (including AI)

AI systems may be used freely for routine tasks: cleaning archives, initial mapping, drafting documentation, and rough analysis.

Hard limits:

The final line must remain understandable and continuable by a human with basic tools.

The line must not become dependent on any particular AI system or cloud infrastructure.

Even when AI has generated substantial portions of the documentation or supporting material, a competent human must still be able to restore and continue the line using only basic tools and the artifacts themselves.

The decisive test is a recovery exercise performed without powerful external AI. If the line fails this test, it does not yet meet the protocol’s standard of survivability.

Practical next step

If the protocol resonates with you:

Choose one small existing piece of work in a Phase 0 domain.

Describe it as an Inheritance Line (decisions, interfaces, constraints, how to verify it).

Remove unnecessary material.

Honestly evaluate it against understandability and isolation resistance.

If you decide on a major rewrite, explicitly record how you will compensate the Line Break.

Start there. Everything else can follow from real attempts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from good documentation and open source?

They are necessary but not sufficient. SPRP adds binding requirements for continuity, understandability without the original authors, isolation resistance, and mandatory compensation when existing lines are broken.

Is this going to create heavy bureaucracy?

There is no central authority. Participants apply the principles themselves. Early application is mostly qualitative. The point is to ask the right questions consistently, not to produce perfect scores.

What if I work inside a company and cannot open everything?

The protocol is voluntary. Its discipline (preserving lines, reducing unnecessary breaks, improving internal understandability) can be applied inside closed environments. The strongest effects appear in open lines.

Why only three domains at the start?

To prevent dilution and to force the creation of real working examples before expanding. Scope expands only after demonstrated results and successful recovery exercises.

How do I recognise a Line Break?

Continuing interfaces, contracts, core constraints and verification methods equals development of the line. Discarding working solutions in favour of an incompatible replacement with no practical way back equals a Line Break. When uncertain, the protocol favours compensation.

How strictly must the Validity Vector be calculated?

Not strictly at the beginning. Use the five criteria as a thinking framework. Formal measurement methods will mature with experience and will incorporate defined thresholds and independent verification. Recovery exercises provide the primary empirical anchor from the outset.

This seems slower than normal development.

In the short term it often will be. The protocol deliberately optimises for knowledge that compounds over 10–20 years instead of resetting.

Note on AI as a reference tool

Contemporary AI systems can usefully serve as a reference and thinking aid for this protocol. Because the protocol is written in explicit, structured language, most major current models can answer questions about its rules, help clarify concepts, and offer practical suggestions on application. They are supporting tools, not participants.

Summary

SPRP is a voluntary discipline for reducing technical amnesia. It replaces the default of repeated clean-slate rewrites with a structured preference for living, understandable, isolation-resistant Inheritance Lines.

You may continue working as before. Or you may test these rules on one real piece of work and see what happens.
